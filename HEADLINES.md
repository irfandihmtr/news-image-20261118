## 4. UN warns of more cold deaths in Gaza as winter sets in
- What: UN agencies warned that winter rains are worsening Gaza's shelter,
  sanitation and food security crisis. More than 1.7 million people remain
  displaced and about 1.4 million face acute hunger; WFP cut hot meals in tent
  camps from one a day to one every two days. One baby reportedly died of
  hypothermia, five people were killed when a sandbank collapsed, and two
  children died in accidental tent fires. Australia, Canada, Italy and Britain
  jointly urged Israel to remove obstacles to humanitarian deliveries.

## 5. COP31 enters its decisive phase in Antalya
- What: Negotiators at the COP31 climate talks in Antalya are working through
